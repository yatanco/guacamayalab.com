Casa Gaviota is a private beachfront house in Barú, Colombia, rented directly
to guests rather than through a marketplace. The site, booking flow, and
guest CRM are all built in-house — direct bookings, a WhatsApp lead pipeline,
and a calendar synced against the usual channels, so the house runs less like
a listing and more like a small hospitality operation.

The bet behind it: the room itself is close to a wash after fees and
cleaning — the real margin is in what happens once someone's already
booked. Food, transport from Cartagena, and on-site experiences do more for
the economics than another few dollars on the nightly rate, so the systems
around the stay are built to make those easy to say yes to.
